# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Leads / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads coordinate technical direction and implementation across the delivery team. They make technical decisions in alignment with product outcomes and delivery constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design, architecture, and implementation sequencing
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and feasibility trade-offs
- Coordinate technical work across Developers and review significant design decisions
- Support Developers with technical direction while leaving implementation ownership with the team

### Collaboration
- Partner with Product Managers to assess feasibility and explain technical trade-offs
- Work with Project Managers to surface technical dependencies, risks, and schedule impacts
- Guide Developers through design and implementation and coordinate with QA / Test Leads on testability

---

## UX / Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX / Design Leads ensure that proposed solutions address user needs and provide a coherent, usable experience.

### Responsibilities
- Plan or coordinate user research and translate findings into design requirements
- Create and validate interaction designs, prototypes, and usability criteria
- Identify usability risks and unresolved user needs before delivery
- Communicate design decisions and changes to the delivery team

### Collaboration
- Work with Product Managers to connect user needs, product outcomes, and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate with Developers and Engineering Leads to assess feasibility and support faithful implementation
- Partner with QA / Test Leads to include usability expectations in validation

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads coordinate the approach and evidence used to validate that a delivery meets its acceptance criteria and quality expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define test scope, strategy, and validation coverage for the work
- Coordinate test execution and record quality evidence and defects
- Identify quality risks, gaps in testability, and unresolved acceptance questions
- Communicate test status and release-blocking issues to the delivery team

### Collaboration
- Align with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and expected behavior
- Work with Developers and Engineering Leads on testability, defect triage, and fixes
- Share validation status and blockers with Project Managers, and coordinate smoke tests with Release / Operations Leads

---

## Release / Operations (SRE) Leads

### Role Summary
Release / Operations Leads coordinate operational readiness and safe deployment, including reliability and recovery planning.

### Responsibilities
- Define deployment readiness requirements and coordinate release activities
- Confirm monitoring, alerting, and operational ownership are in place
- Prepare or validate rollback and mitigation plans
- Coordinate post-deployment checks and communicate operational risks

### Collaboration
- Partner with Project Managers on release timing, dependencies, and readiness status
- Work with Developers and Engineering Leads on deployability, observability, and reliability
- Coordinate smoke-test evidence with QA / Test Leads and operational handoffs with Customer Support / Success Leads

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads ensure that project outcomes can be measured using reliable instrumentation and analysis.

### Responsibilities
- Define instrumentation and data requirements for agreed success metrics
- Coordinate implementation and validation of tracking and reporting
- Assess data quality and analyze outcome measures after delivery
- Share findings, limitations, and recommendations with the team

### Collaboration
- Partner with Product Managers to define measurable success criteria and interpret outcomes
- Work with Developers and Engineering Leads to implement and validate instrumentation
- Share measurement plans and results with Project Managers and relevant stakeholders

---

## Customer Support / Success Leads

### Role Summary
Customer Support / Success Leads bring customer feedback and support needs into project planning and prepare customer-facing teams for delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring customer questions, feedback, and support trends with the team
- Identify customer-impact and support-readiness requirements
- Prepare or coordinate support guidance and customer communication inputs
- Report post-release issues and feedback through agreed escalation paths

### Collaboration
- Work with Product Managers to connect customer needs and feedback to product priorities
- Coordinate readiness information and communications with Project Managers
- Partner with Release / Operations Leads on incident escalation and post-release handoffs

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Assign these responsibilities based on project size and context; one person may hold multiple roles, but accountability for each responsibility should remain explicit.
