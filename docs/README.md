# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder brings together OctoAcme's project management processes, roles, and practices.

## Project management processes at a glance

OctoAcme follows an iterative lifecycle. Initiation confirms the business need, measurable outcomes, stakeholders, risks, and whether work should proceed. Planning turns approved work into a prioritized, estimated backlog with acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, dependencies, and milestones. Teams execute in small increments, track progress on a project board, and review results regularly. Releases require readiness checks and post-deployment verification; retrospectives capture lessons and track improvement actions.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communications; Product Managers define outcomes, prioritize work, and measure impact. Developers build, test, document, and review changes, QA validates acceptance criteria, and stakeholders provide input and approvals.

Teams communicate through delivery standups, weekly PM and Product Manager alignment, regular stakeholder updates, and demos or reviews at the end of a sprint or milestone. Blockers and dependencies are raised in team forums and escalated through the PM, Product Lead, and sponsor as needed. Quality is supported by unit tests for new logic, integration tests where applicable, smoke tests for critical flows, CI tests and linting, security scans, and review before merge. Manual QA is used when feature acceptance requires it.
